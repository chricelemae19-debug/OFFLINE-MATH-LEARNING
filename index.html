    About Math Adventure

    Math Adventure is an offline interactive Grade 3 Mathematics learning system organized according to the weekly competencies of the First, Second, and Third Terms.

    📗 First Term — Weeks 1–10
    📘 Second Term — Weeks 1–10
    📕 Third Term — Weeks 1–10
    📖 Weekly Topic Presentation
    🎯 4 Activity Levels
    🔘 Multiple Choice Activities
    🧠 Mathematics Practice
    🔊 Sound and Voice Feedback
    📊 My Progress
    📱 Mobile Friendly
    💾 Offline Learning Support
    ❓

    How to Use Math Adventure

    1. Login
    Enter your name to start.
    2. Choose a Term
    Select First, Second, or Third Term.
    3. Choose a Week
    Select one of the 10 weekly topics.
    4. Study the Topic Presentation
    Read the teacher-ready lesson presentation from beginning to end.
    5. Choose Activity Level
    Choose Beginner, Explorer, Challenger, or Math Master.
    6. Answer 10 Questions
    Read each question carefully and select your answer.
    7. Check Your Result
    Your score will be displayed after the activity.
    8. View My Progress
    Your completed activities and scores are saved.
